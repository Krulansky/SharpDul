## Blender / 3D art track (added 2026-11-14)

The director's career goal is GameDev **and** 3D art, and the Q1 2027 plan gives 3D art about
12 h a week, so the study path gets a second track next to the Unity courses. Courses are not
chosen yet. The weak areas, in the order a game asset needs them, are:

1. topology and retopology
2. UVs
3. texturing
4. materials and shading
5. lighting
6. game-ready export to Unity (scale, pivot, axes - see FerryHeist `ART_SPEC.md`)

Strengths: modeling, some animation and basic rigging. Blender 5.2. Practice on real game
assets (Riddle of Steel), not on separate experiments. The director knows they tend to
binge-learn Blender, so keep the track tied to deliverables.
